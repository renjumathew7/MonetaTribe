1. Introduction

Welcome to MonetaTribe!

MonetaTribe is a collaborative family budgeting application designed to help families manage their finances, track income and expenses, create budgets, monitor savings and work towards shared financial goals.

MonetaTribe is operated by Nayron Labs ("we", "our" or "us").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and manage personal information when you access or use our website and web application.

We are committed to respecting your privacy and handling your personal and financial information responsibly.

By using MonetaTribe, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to provide and maintain our services.

2.1 Account Information

When you register for MonetaTribe, we may collect:

  • Full name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Password credentials, stored and managed using appropriate security measures.
  • Profile picture or selected avatar.
  • Account creation date and related account information.

MonetaTribe currently uses email and password authentication.

2.2 Financial Information

MonetaTribe allows users to manually record and manage their household finances.

This information may include:

  • Income records.
  • Expense records.
  • Transaction descriptions and dates.
  • Budget categories and allocations.
  • Savings targets.
  • Financial goals.
  • Spending notes and other voluntarily entered financial information.

Manual Data Entry: Users enter their financial information themselves. MonetaTribe does not currently connect directly to bank accounts or automatically retrieve bank transactions.

We do not require users to provide bank account passwords, ATM PINs or card security codes for the application's budgeting functionality.

2.3 Family and Household Information

MonetaTribe allows users to create family groups and collaborate with other family members.

We may collect:

  • Family or household names.
  • Family member names.
  • Family roles and relationships, where provided.
  • Member profiles and avatars.
  • Group membership and permissions.
  • Shared budgets and financial records.
  • Contributions and savings activities.

Information shared within a family group may be accessible to other authorised members according to their assigned permissions and application settings.

2.4 Children's Profiles

Parents or authorised guardians may create and manage profiles for their children within a family group.

Such profiles may contain:

  • Child's name or nickname.
  • Age or date of birth, if provided.
  • Avatar or profile image.
  • Assigned family role.
  • Budget allocations and financial activities.
  • Savings goals and progress.

Parents or authorised guardians are responsible for managing children's participation and determining what information is shared within their family group.

We handle children's information with additional care and apply applicable parental consent and child-data protection requirements.

2.5 Technical Information

When you access MonetaTribe, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Login timestamps.
  • Application usage and diagnostic information.
  • Error reports and security-related information.

The collection of this information may depend on our hosting infrastructure and application configuration.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  1. Create and manage user accounts.
  2. Authenticate users through email and password.
  3. Create and manage family groups.
  4. Maintain family member profiles and permissions.
  5. Record and display manually entered income and expenses.
  6. Organise budgets and spending categories.
  7. Track savings goals and financial progress.
  8. Provide collaborative budgeting features.
  9. Maintain application security and prevent unauthorised access.
  10. Troubleshoot technical issues.
  11. Improve application functionality and reliability.
  12. Respond to user enquiries and support requests.
  13. Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use your personal financial information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis.

4. Cloud Storage and Data Processing

MonetaTribe uses cloud-based database infrastructure to store and process application information.

Information submitted through the application may be transmitted to and stored on cloud servers operated by us or our third-party infrastructure providers.

This may include:

  • Account and profile information.
  • Family group information.
  • Financial records.
  • Budget and savings information.
  • Application-related technical data.

We may use third-party cloud hosting, database, authentication, storage and infrastructure providers to operate MonetaTribe.

These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to deliver the service.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that service providers handling personal information are subject to appropriate confidentiality, security and data protection obligations.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal or financial information to third parties.

Information may be shared in the following circumstances:

5.1 Within Family Groups

Financial records and other information intentionally shared within a family group may be visible to authorised members based on their roles and permissions.

Users should understand that information shared within a family group may be viewed by other authorised participants.

5.2 Third-Party Service Providers

We may share necessary information with trusted service providers involved in:

  • Cloud hosting and database management.
  • User authentication.
  • Data storage and backup.
  • Email delivery.
  • Security monitoring.
  • Technical maintenance.

These providers are permitted to process information only as necessary to provide their contracted services, subject to applicable safeguards.

5.3 Legal and Regulatory Requirements

We may disclose information when required by applicable laws, regulations, court orders or lawful governmental requests.

5.4 Business Transfers

If Nayron Labs undergoes a merger, acquisition, restructuring or transfer of business assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable laws.

6. Children's Privacy

MonetaTribe is designed to support family financial management, including the participation of children through parent-managed profiles.

Parents or authorised guardians may create and manage children's profiles within their family groups.

We recognise that children's personal information requires additional protection.

Accordingly:

  • Children's profiles should be created and managed by an authorised parent or guardian.
  • Parents or guardians should provide any consent required under applicable laws.
  • We do not intend to use children's information for targeted advertising.
  • We do not knowingly permit children's information to be used for purposes unrelated to the application's functionality without an appropriate legal basis.
  • Parents or guardians may request access, correction or deletion of their children's information, subject to applicable laws.

Where required by law, we will implement appropriate age verification and parental consent mechanisms.

7. Cookies and Session Management

MonetaTribe may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential application functionality.

These may be used for:

  • Maintaining authenticated user sessions.
  • Remembering application preferences.
  • Supporting security mechanisms.
  • Improving application reliability.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may prevent certain application features from functioning correctly.

8. Analytics and Tracking

At present, our analytics and tracking arrangements have not been finalised.

If we introduce analytics or similar technologies in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy to explain:

  • What information is collected.
  • Why the information is collected.
  • Which service providers may process it.
  • How users can manage applicable privacy choices.

We will obtain consent wherever required by applicable law.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information stored and processed through MonetaTribe.

These measures may include:

  • Authentication and access controls.
  • Appropriate password security practices.
  • Secure communication protocols.
  • Database access restrictions.
  • Security monitoring.
  • Appropriate backup and recovery procedures.

Access to information is intended to be limited to authorised users and personnel who require it to operate or maintain the service.

Although we take reasonable precautions, no internet-based application or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Users should maintain the confidentiality of their login credentials and notify us if they suspect unauthorised access to their accounts.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services and fulfil legitimate operational and legal requirements.

Information may be retained while:

  • A user account remains active.
  • A family group is operational.
  • Financial records are required to provide application functionality.
  • Legal obligations require retention.
  • Disputes or security investigations remain unresolved.

Users may request account closure and deletion of their personal information by contacting us.

Where an account or family profile is deleted, we will take reasonable steps to delete associated information, subject to legal retention requirements, technical limitations and applicable backup-retention procedures.

Information that has been shared with other family members may already have been viewed or retained by those members.

  • Request access to their personal information.

  • Raise complaints regarding privacy-related concerns.
